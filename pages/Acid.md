There are two types of acids:
- Bronsted-Lowry acid: a molecule or ion that is capable of donating a proton (i.e. a hydrogen cation)
- [Lewis acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_acid): a molecule or ion that is capable of forming a covalent bond with an electron pair from another molecule/ion

An aqueous solution of acid will have a sour taste, which influenced its name - the Latin word "acidus" means "sour".

An aqueous solution of acid will also have a pH of less than 7, because pH is inversely proportional to the concentration of Hydrogen cations and acids produce Hydrogen cations.
