- See [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breadboard).
- Breadboards are just strips of wire with plastic holes on top.
- Wires can be measured with AWG (American Wire Gauge).
- How to strip wires: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fDo-KbKRd4A
- # Best Practices
	- Use red wire for power.
	- Use black wire for ground.
	- Keep your wires the exact length you need them to be so they are as flat as possible and don't catch on anything.
	- Keep your wires "Manhattan style" - running in parallel or at 90 degrees to each other, no diagonal wires.
