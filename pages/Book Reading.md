### Good methods for acquiring books

E-books:
- Library accounts (connect online with Libby)
- [LibGen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_Genesis) (see [Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_Genesis) for current mirror URLs)
	- Also see the [Wiki](https://www.reddit.com/r/libgen/wiki/index/)[Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) (also see [Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Archive))
