General notes related to cars.

### Replacing 12V battery
Disconnect the negative (black) terminal first, because it’s also connected to the car ground and could complete a shorted circuit if the positive (red) terminal touched ground once disconnected.

### Jump Starting a Car
Risks:
- Short-circuiting the good battery and damaging it
	- This can occur if you create a low-resistance conductive path (like a normal wire) between the good battery's terminals
- Igniting hydrogen gas with a spark, causing an explosion
	- Hydrogen gas can be produced by either battery, but the dead battery is more likely to have lingering gas (a battery that is functioning nominally produces less gas)

Note:
- Red means positive (+)
- Black means negative (-)
- **Red clamps connect to red battery terminals on both sides, and black clamps connect to black battery terminals on both sides**The black/negative battery terminal is connected to the car ground, which usually includes the metal chassis

Procedure:

**Start with both car engines off, to reduce the amount of activity / moving parts during setup.**

Connect cables in this order:
1. **Red (+) clamp on dead battery**	- No spark risk since battery is dead
	- Reds first since they are not connected to ground/chassis, meaning it is more difficult to accidentally create a short-circuit to the good battery's red terminal
2. **Red (+) clamp on good battery**
	- Completes the red link while minimizing the chance of accidentally completing a circuit
3. **Black (-) clamp on good battery**
	- The less we have to deal with the dead battery's terminals directly, the better
4. **Black (-) clamp on ground/chassis of car with dead battery, as far away from dead battery as possible**	- Black terminal is connected to ground, so this completes the circuit, which can generate a spark
	- Connecting to a distant ground, rather than the black terminal on the dead battery, keeps a potential spark away from any flammable gases produced by the dead battery

Then, start the good car's engine, to charge the dead battery (and keep its own battery charged).

Wait a few minutes, then try starting the dead car's engine. If it starts, you can disconnect the cables in the reverse order that you connected them.
