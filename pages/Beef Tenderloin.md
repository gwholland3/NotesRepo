Notice how the tenderloin portion is not uniform in thickness. It is pretty uniform in the center, but tapers off on both ends into a skinnier tail.
A center cut beef tenderloin is therefore the center portion of the tenderloin, cut and separated from the tail ends so that you're left with a cylindrical shaped piece of meat.

You can then slice that center cut into slabs, which are called filet mignon steaks.

An unprocessed cut of tenderloin will often have some silverskin on the outside. Silverskin is a tough, fibrous connective tissue that you don't want to eat. Therefore, it should be trimmed off with a knife prior to cooking the cut.
