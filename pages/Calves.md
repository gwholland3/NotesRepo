### Cankles
"[Cankles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cankles)" are a slang term referring to a condition where a person's ankles lack definition from their calves.

Possible causes of candles:
- Standing too much: blood pools in the ankles, making them appear more swollen than normal
- Swollen ankle: just a normal 'ole swollen ankle
- Obesity: a chubby ankle will have a smoother transition to the calf
- Edema: a medical condition where fluid builds up in the body's tissue
- Poor blood pumping: normally the heart needs to pump blood to get it to circulate back up against gravity from the ankles
