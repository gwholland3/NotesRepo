## Specific Kits

### Rolife Cathy's Flower House
I got this kit from Rolife: [https://www.rolifeonline.com/products/rolife-cathys-flower-house-dg104-diy-miniature-dollhouse](https://www.rolifeonline.com/products/rolife-cathys-flower-house-dg104-diy-miniature-dollhouse)

Things not included that I need:
- Small metal pliers
- Small clips for holding things together while glue dries, such as:
	- Binder clips
- Toothpick for applying glue
- Better glue?
- Needle and thread
- Two AAA batteries

Notable things from the website:
- "you can easily purchase craft glue from any local store or online"
- "Cathy’s Flower House comes pre-painted, so you do not need to paint any of the pieces yourself"
