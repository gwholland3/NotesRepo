An electrolyte is simply a substance that conducts electricity (moves electric charge) through the movement of ions rather than the movement of electrons.

Athletes tend to lose electrolytes quickly when they sweat.

The main electrolytes found in the human body are sodium, potassium, magnesium, chloride, hydrogen phosphate, and hydrogen carbonate.

Excess levels of electrolytes are typically flushed out by the kidneys.
