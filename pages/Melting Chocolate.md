### Method 1: Double Boiler
Get some water simmering in a pot/pan on the stove. Then, place a heatproof bowl on top of the pot/pan, such that it fits snuggly and doesn't touch the water. Finally, add the chocolate to the bowl, and stir it as it melts.

### Method 2: Microwave
Put the chocolate in a microwave-save bowl, then microwave it in 30-second increments, stirring in between each time, until it is the desired consistency.
