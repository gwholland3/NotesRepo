## **ION Radar seat harness**
I believe this is the product I have (I purchased it at a store): [link](https://www.ion-products.com/en/us/products/harness-seat-kite-radar-48242-4720)

Instructions on tag:
- Wash your harness regularly with fresh water and store dry. It will last longer
- Allow to drip in the shade avoiding direct sunlight.
- When not in use, store dry in a cool shaded environment
- Always rinse in fresh water after use. If necessary, use a mild detergent.
- Keep an eye on the metal parts, clean off sand and salt.

## 2025 13 meter Duotone Evo kite
I believe this is the product I have (I purchased it at a store): [link](https://www.duotonesports.com/en/us/products/duotone-evo-2025-44250-3003?variant=47192028971240)

This is probably the most relevant manual: [link](https://a.storyblok.com/f/120582/x/cabb171899/dtk25_mnl_kite-general_en_2025_www.pdf)

Maintenance tips from the manual:
- Avoid bringing the kite into contact with sharp-edged rocks, thorns, wood and other objects that may damage, puncture or tear the cloth.
- Do not expose the kite to excessive sun as it ages the material unnecessarily.
- Deflate the bladders if the kite is to be left unattended for any length of time.
- Avoid leaving your kite on the beach for extended lengths of time as small wind movements may quickly cause wear and tear to the cloth.
- Try to pack up the kite without cuttlings into the kitebag to avoid tearing the canopy.
- To carry a kite, turn it so that the leading edge is upwind and the underside faces upwards. Grab the centre of the leading edge tube. If the wind is blowing, it will hold the kite clear of the ground. Be sure not to drag the tips of the kite across the ground.
- A coating that results from using the kite in salt water will not damage the kite. However, rinsing in fresh water and drying doesn’t hurt.
- When cleaning the kite be sure that no water, dirt, or sand gets inside the bladders.
- Be sure that the kite has dried completely before packing it up. Otherwise changes of color or mildew could occur. Such bleeding is not covered under the DUOTONE warranty.
- Do not dry the kite in direct sun!
- Keep sand and water out of the air pump

Maintenance tips from other kiteboarder:
- Roll your kite up on the cement, not on the sand. That way it stays less sandy (the sand is abrasive)
	- The cement can be abrasive, too. Make sure you aren't dragging the kite on the cement at all
