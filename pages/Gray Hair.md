## What Causes Normal Hair Color?
Each strand of your hair is produced by a [hair follicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_follicle) organ.

Normally, cells in your hair follicles called melanocytes produce melanin. It is this melanin that gives your hair its color. The more melanocytes you have in your hair follicles, the stronger your hair color will be.


## What Is Happening When Hair Turns Gray?
When the concentration of melanin in a hair strand decreases, that strand will appear more translucent.

### Stress
Stress can trigger a condition called telogen effluvium. This doesn’t *directly* cause hair to change color, but it does make your hair shed ~3x faster than normal.


## Can Gray Hair Become Colored Again?
Yes.

In some cases, if whatever is decreasing the concentration of melanin in a particular hair strand is eliminated, the concentration can return to normal levels again.

For example, suppose some kind of internal or external stress starts killing off melanocyte cells faster than they can be replaced by parent/stem cells. If that stress is removed, and there are enough parent/stem cells, they can replenish the lost melanocyte cells, which will resume producing melanin and restore its concentration.
