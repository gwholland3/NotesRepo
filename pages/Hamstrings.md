A hamstring is a muscle running down the rear of the thigh, connecting the hip and the knee.

Everyone has three distinct hamstring muscles on each leg:
- [Semimembranosus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semimembranosus)[Semitendinosus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semitendinosus)[Biceps femoris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biceps_femoris)
All three muscles participate in flexing the knee joint, and the first two extend the hip joint.

The hamstrings contract when the knee is flexed, and they lengthen when either the knee or the hip is extended. That means they also lengthen when the hip joint is bent.

The hamstrings are part of the [posterior chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posterior_chain), which also includes the calves and glutes.

## Injury

One common injury is Proximal Hamstring Tendinopathy (PHT), usually a result of irritation or micro-damage where the hamstrings attach to the ischial tuberosity (aka "sit bone"). It's often caused by repetitive stress, such as running.
