Good keyboard shortcuts to remember:

Deletion:

- CTRL-w  - delete a word backwards
	- Alternative: ESC-<backspace>
- ESC-d  - delete a word forwards
- CTRL-u  - delete an entire line backwards
- CTRL-k  - delete an entire line forwards
  
  Other:
- CTRL-_  - undo an action
