## Grocery Shopping
Good strategy:
- Keep running list of items needed, add to throughout the week
- Check if any items require specific grocery stores before heading out
- Buy everything on list

I think some grocery stores offer pickup programs. That could safe me a lot of time each week, if it’s free. Let’s go down the list of nearby grocery stores and check.

### Fred Meyers
[Their website](https://www.fredmeyer.com/i/ways-to-shop/pickup) says that pickup is free for orders of $35 or more, and my grocery bill is almost always at least that much.

### Trader Joes’s
No pickup available.

### Safeway
[This Safeway webpage](https://www.safeway.com/lp/grocery-pickup.html) says they do offer pickup. [This website](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/save-money/grocery-pick-up/) says it’s free on orders of $30 or more.

### Vons
[This Vons webpage](https://www.vons.com/lp/curbside-pickup.html) says they do offer pickup. There is a $3.95 fee for orders under $30 (discovered through experimentation).

### Target
[This Target webpage](https://www.target.com/c/order-pickup/-/N-ng0a0) indicates they do offer pickup. From searching the web, there does not appear to be a minimum grocery bill amount for the service to be free.

### QFC
[Their website](https://www.qfc.com/i/ways-to-shop/pickup) says that pickup is free for orders of $35 or more.
