## **Fillings**

Among other things, fillings are used to address:
- Tooth decay
- Cavities
- Minor tooth damage


Fillings can come in different materials:
- Amalgam/silver fillings:
	- Durable, work well for molars and heavily-used teeth
	- Metal-colored
	- Older technique, slowly being phased out of modern dentistry (mostly because it contains mercury)
	- The filling hole in the tooth needs to be in a box shape, which can require removing excess healthy tooth
- Composite fillings:
	- Made of plastic resin and glass
	- Blend in with tooth color
- Gold/porcelain fillings:
	- Extremely durable (gold more expensive than porcelain, though)
- Glass ionomer fillings:
	- Versatile


Filling Procedure ([source](https://mintdentalmn.com/step-by-step-dental-filling-procedure/)):
- Numbing the area surrounding the tooth
- Remove the existing decay, via scraping and drilling
	- Check to make sure nothing was missed with an indicator dye
- Select filling material
- Place the filling material
- Allow the filling to set/harden
	- Time-based for some filling materials, requires curing light for others
- Adjust the shape of the filling’s exterior to align with your bite
	- Any discomfortable spots are ground down
- Polish/finish the filling


## Tooth Problems

[Plaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_plaque) is basically just first-stage gunk that accumulates on your teeth, mostly consisting of bacteria. It can progress into tartar and tooth decay if not addressed.

Dental caries, tooth decay, and cavities are all synonyms for the same thing.


## Retainers
My current upper retainer is a Hawley retainer: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retainer_(orthodontics)#Hawley_retainers)
My current lower retainer is a fixed retainer ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retainer_(orthodontics)#Fixed_retainers)) behind my four front teeth.

My current LA dentist at Pearl Dental El Segundo, Connor Dolce, recommends I get [vacuum-formed retainers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retainer_(orthodontics)#Vacuum-formed_retainers) for both upper and lower.
This is because they make it easier to clean my teeth and provide better protection against rotational tooth drift.

Questions:
- Does the vacuum-formed retainer wear down quicker than the Hawley retainer?
	- No, says Connor
