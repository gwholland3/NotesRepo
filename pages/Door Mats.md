## Outdoor Doormat
An outdoor doormat is intended for shoe scraping, to remove chunks of dirt and other debris.

Best materials for outdoor doormats:
- Traditional or synthetic coir mats
- Rubber mats with holes
- Low profile scrapers with elevated grippers
- Drainable PVC mats

## Indoor Doormat
An indoor doormat is intended to remove any last bits of smaller dirt and absorb any remaining moisture from shoes, to avoid wet floors.

Best materials for indoor doormats:
- Carpet-surface entrance mats
- Coir mats with rubber backing
- Aluminum scrapers
