Wikipedia: [link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wart)

Most often caused by [human papillomavirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_papillomavirus).

## Treatment
See [WikiHow](https://www.wikihow.com/Get-Rid-of-Warts-on-Hands).

Most common approach:
- Soak the wart in warm water for 5-10 minutes to soften the skin up
- Scrub the wart slightly with a nail file or other exfoliator (like pumice)
	- This wears away the surface of the wart and exposes it to deeper penetration by medication
	- The abrasion also stimulates the immune system to be more active in eliminating the wart
- Apply [salicylic acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salicylic_acid) to the wart
	- Look for an over-the-counter treatment containing 17-40% salicylic acid
- Cover the wart with tape or a bandage
	- Duct tape works well for this

The above should be performed daily for several weeks.
