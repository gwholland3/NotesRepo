Maximum recommended continuous use: 1 hour
(After that, take a 20-minute break to allow for heat dissipation before using again)

The remote controller uses a CR2032 3V button cell battery.

### Leg Posts
The leg posts screw into a ring that's roughly 5/8 inches in diameter. The screws themselves are roughly 5/16 inches in diameter.
I'll want spacers that are 3/16 inch long/tall. The interior hole diameter should be > 5/16 inch, and the outer diameter should be 1/2 inch to 7/8 inch

### Maintenance
To re-lubricate the WalkingPad, apply 5-10 ml of [silicone oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicone_oil) in a Z shape underneath the belt, then activate the WalkingPad to spread the oil around.

According to [this page](https://www.walkingpad.com/blogs/walkingpad-news/lubricate-the-belt-for-walkingpad-treadmill), I should re-lubricate every three months.

I've purchased [this silicone oil](https://www.amazon.com/Lubricants-Temperature-Resistant-Lubricant-Treadmill/dp/B0DCBS88HQ).

To tune the belt alignment:
- If the belt slips to the right, turn the rightmost hole clockwise
- If the belt slips to the left, turn the leftmost hole clockwise
For both sides, turning clockwise moves that side of the belt further from the base (stretches the belt).
