Source: [VectorVest](https://www.vectorvest.com/blog/market-timing/can-you-trade-stocks-on-the-weekend/)

Standard business hours for "the stock market" are Monday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Time.

This means that stocks can't be bought or sold (at least through official stock exchanges) over the weekend. You can submit buy or sell orders, but they won't execute until market open on Monday.
