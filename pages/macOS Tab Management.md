## Native
Most native functionality comes from [Mission Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission_Control_(macOS)) and other built-in keyboard shortcuts.

Things to remember:
- CTRL-<down_arrow>  - activate App Exposé:
	- ESC  - exit without changing windows
	- CTRL-<up_arrow> or ENTER  - open the currently-selected window
	- Arrow keys: navigate through the current app's windows
	- TAB and SHIFT-TAB  - cycle through open apps
- CMD-TAB, then release TAB only  - activate app switcher. While CMD is still held down:
	- ESC  - exit without changing apps
	- Release CMD  - open the currently-selected app
	- (TAB or <right_arrow>) and (SHIFT-TAB or ` or <left_arrow>)  - cycle through open apps
	- <down_arrow> or <up_arrow> or 1  - open App Exposé for the currently-selected app
