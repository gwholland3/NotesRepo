### Cleaning
First, try to shake out any snow, water, or ice. Then let it dry for a bit.

When cleaning the lenses, you want to do it differently for the outer and inner lenses.

For the outer lens, use a microfiber cloth to rub any stains away.

The inner lens typically has a very fancy anti-fog coating that is sensitive to physical contact. Therefore, try to avoid having to clean the inner lens in the first place, if you want to keep that coating intact.
