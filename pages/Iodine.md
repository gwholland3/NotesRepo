Iodine is an element with atomic number 53.

There is correlation between adequate iodine levels and cognition/intelligence. Iodized salt is one of the main ways governments promote iodine consumption for their citizens.

[Iodine deficiency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iodine_deficiency) can cause lower intelligence, wow!
