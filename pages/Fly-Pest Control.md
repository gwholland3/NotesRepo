

- ## Flies
  
  Source: [https://www.wikihow.com/Get-Rid-of-Flies-in-the-House](https://www.wikihow.com/Get-Rid-of-Flies-in-the-House)
- ### Preventing Fly Presence
  
  Passive:
- Spreading fly repellant on surfaces (can also kill flies), e.g.:
	- Essential oils
- Fly-repellant plants, e.g.:
	- Basil
	- Bay leaf
	- Mint
	- Rosemary
	- Lavender
- Use a garbage can with a fly-proof lid
- Ultrasonic pest repellers
	- [This](https://www.amazon.com/Lickoon-Ultrasonic-Electronic-Repellent-Warehouse/dp/B0CKYJ55F7/) is what my former roommate bought
	  
	  Active:
- Clean up food and food waste quickly
	- Rinse sink and run garbage disposal after washing dishes
- Clean sink drains periodically, with:
	- Drain snakes
	- Drain cleaners
- Install screens on windows and doors, and repair any tears/damage
- Fill in cracks and crevices with caulk
- Burn fly-repellant candles, e.g. citronella-scented ones
- ### Fighting Fly Presence
  
  Passive:
- Fly traps, e.g.:
	- Bowl of vinegar covered with plastic wrap with holes in it
		- Add dish soap to disrupt the vinegar’s surface tension and ensure that flies get stuck upon contact
	- Light traps
	- Fly tape
	  
	  Active:
- Fly swatter
- Spraying insecticide at flies, e.g.:
	- Diatomaceous earth
	- Pyrethrin spray
