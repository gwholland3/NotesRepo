## Donation
Check for nearby blood donation opportunities: [https://www.aabb.org/for-donors-patients/give-blood](https://www.aabb.org/for-donors-patients/give-blood)

The American Red Cross Blood Donation Center in Torrance is close to me: [website](https://www.redcrossblood.org/give.html/drive-results?zipSponsor=90277)
There's also the UCLA Blood & Platelet Center: [website](https://www.uclahealth.org/programs/gotblood/make-appointment)

It is generally not recommended to give blood soon after exercising, because you risk dehydration and dizziness.

### Types

Whole blood donation collects red blood cells, plasma, and platelets. It takes about eight minutes on average and can be done every 56 days.

A power red donation collects twice as many red blood cells as normal, and returns the plasma and platelets to the donor. It takes around 20-30 minutes on average and can be done every four months. There are stricter requirements for which donors can perform power red donations.

A platelet donation only collects platelets. It takes about two hours and can be done about every two weeks.
